The pictures agree; the distributions do not. At matched epochs the two trainers land within ~0.05 dB of each other on this image, while nerc is consistently 0.25–0.32 better on COCO rFID wherever both are measured.

Likeliest cause, stated as a hypothesis: LDM trains on min_side × U(0.5, 1.0) random crops, nerc on full squashed images. vae_bench evaluates full images, so nerc's training distribution sits closer to the eval distribution. fp16-vs-fp32 and the optimiser settings are the other candidates; none of this is tested yet.

Both degrade from the shared 10.481 init, and both do it almost entirely in the first epoch or two, then flatten.
